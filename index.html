Under Review is an anomaly-hunt horror game with escape-room mechanics and a mystery story evolving in the background. Inspect uncanny hotel rooms, document discrepancies and survive the strict, unforgiving bureaucracy of the Manager.
The Liminal Hotel
You wake up in an elevator, then step out into a quiet hotel lobby outside of time and space. Overseeing you from behind a window is the Manager. To earn your way out you must memorize Room 1 and review the remaining 6.
The Review
Each room can have none, one, or more anomalies across four categories: Displacement, Surplus, Condition Variance and Presence. For each room you get a form that you submit for review to the Manager. Only two mistakes per floor allowed.
Solve Puzzles
Escape-room style puzzles are layered throughout the game as well as a mysterious story that keeps evolving as the Manager piles more and more work for you. Find clues and solve puzzles to find your way to one of the three endings.
